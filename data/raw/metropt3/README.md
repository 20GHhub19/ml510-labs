# MetroPT-3 raw data

Place the official 2020 `MetroPT3(AirCompressor).csv` here without editing it. Follow the [S6 acquisition and verification guide](../../../docs/s6_unsupervised.md#2-obtain-and-verify-metropt-3).

Davari, Veloso, Ribeiro, and Gama (2021), *MetroPT-3 Dataset*, [DOI: 10.24432/C5VW3R](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5VW3R). Data license: CC BY 4.0, separate from the repository code's MIT license. The CSV and archive remain ignored by Git.
